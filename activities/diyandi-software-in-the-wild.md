## 1. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  
Iliganons and out-of-town visitors alike as attendees wanting to safely navigate and enjoy the month-long Diyandi Festival

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  
These attendees need a reliable as well as accessible way to plan which events they want to attend, track real-time schedules, and ensure their physical safety amid massive, unpredictable crowds and the communications blackouts during major events and parades.

---

## 2. Situation or need

**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**  
To be able to make informed decisions about whether they should attend or at least be aware of the 137 scheduled activities of the festival [1]. They want to be able to locate temporary first aid facilities, avoid suffocating crowds, and effortlessly explore the densly packed venues like the Anahaw Amphitheater or Aguinaldo St. in parades.

---

## 3. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**

The published LGU calendar is confusing and I feel that it underrepresents the 137 actual scheduled events. Additionally, unpredictable crowd density at venues like the Anahaw Amphitheater often deters my friends and me from attending. Furthermore, NTC-mandated mobile network shutdowns during parades [3] disable standard mapping apps, making it difficult to locate first-aid and police outposts offline.

---

## 4. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?**  
I propose a simple interactive Progressive Web App (PWA) of Iligan City's map that provides real-time event tracking, makes venue zooming possible, as well as offline emergency information and navigation.

**How would it help the intended users?**  
It serves as a convenient, accessible, and reliable map guide for Diyandi Festival. It highlights events to help attendees plan efficiently and impulsively. It functions natively during network blackouts, pinpointing nearby first-aid stations and police outposts. For attendees on local Wi-Fi, it displays live event progress and crowd congestion as red zones for the major events of the celebration.

---

## 5. Things users can do

Describe **two specific actions** that users could perform using your proposed system.

1. Check live crowd density to avoid congested venues, and locate emergency stations using the automatic offline map during network blackouts.
2. View real-time map markers and detailed maps of venues like Mugna to easily track simultaneous events and plan for upcoming festival activities of a specific day.

---

## 6. Important qualities

Identify **two qualities** that would make your proposed system useful. You may consider whether it should be easy to use, fast, reliable, safe, private, accessible, multilingual, low-data, clear, or available during high demand.

### Quality 1: Accessible Real-Time Convenience

**Why does this matter to users?**  
Live updates on ongoing events and crowd density remove the anxiety of navigating blind and maximize enjoyment.

### Quality 2: Offline Emergency Reliability

**Why does this matter to users?**  
A PWA architecture will ensure that during total NTC network blackouts, vulnerable attendees can always locate critical safety personnel without relying on cellular data

## 7. How to tell whether the solution helped

How could you determine whether your proposed solution actually helped users?

Surveying attendees or users to see if they actively used the map to plan their routes; checking session usage times to confirm they are  engaging with the features rather than just visiting the app; and asking users in post-event surveys if they found the app useful to want it for next year's Diyandi.
